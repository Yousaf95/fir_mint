## 3. Findings that change the paper or the pilots

1. **A faithful tuner costs seconds, not 1 ms.** C4 cold tuning: 12.0 s (COCO-4 30k), 12.7 s
   (Flickr30k-4), 13.6 s (LAION-1M-4), 49.1 s (COCO-6 30k), 56.1 s (COCO-6 full); calibration
   9-118 s, once. Warm re-tuning removes 83-100% of the tuning time (planner cache kept across
   windows). The draft's 1 ms assumption understates the tuner term by three to four orders of
   magnitude.
2. **The detector's gain in total time is tuner time saved.** Without a tuner cost, re-tuning
   every window under the same ski-rental rule is within 3.6% of FIR+fast+SR. The detector wins by
   5% or more only when a re-tune costs seconds and the window is short (COCO-6 30k and Flickr30k-4
   at Q_w <= 10,000; COCO-6 full at 1,000). The gain shrinks with table size at fixed tuner cost
   (COCO-6: -44.5% at 30k, -14.7% at 117k, Q_w = 1,000), because window query time grows with rows.
3. **The detector prevents churn.** At Q_w = 100,000, Continuous+SR switches 7.8-18.2 times per
   run on the COCO tables, 2.4-5.7 of them before the drift onset; FIR+fast+SR switches at most
   1.75 times with at most 0.03 false switches. Total time in the simulator does not charge this.
4. **C4's recall estimate fails on correlated columns at 1M rows.** C4 estimates every query type
   feasible; measured, captions-only (1,2,3) reaches 0.700 (estimated 0.907) and image + two
   captions (0,1,3) 0.860 (estimated 0.908). Both combine the three caption columns derived from
   the same caption (A1 limitation). Hypothesis: C4 treats misses in different indexes as
   independent. P4 tests it.
5. **On the 4-column tables the configuration does not move; on COCO-6 it does.** C4's drifted
   configuration equals its stable one on all 5 seeds of LAION-1M-4 and COCO-4 30k, and differs on
   5 of 5 (COCO-6 full), 4 of 5 (COCO-6 30k), 3 of 5 (Flickr30k-4). With finding 4: on LAION-1M-4,
   C4 believes the caption types are served by merging single-column searches and never spends
   budget on text indexes. COCO-6 full is the table where re-tuning has something to gain under
   MINT's cost model; this matters for P1b (it must pass on both LAION-1M-4 and COCO-6 full).
6. **Recall-violating mass is not zero beyond seed 4.** In the frozen simulator it is positive on
   4 of 30 seeds on COCO-4 and COCO-6 30k, 2 of 30 on Flickr30k-4 and COCO-6 full, equally for every
   policy including Static. The draft's "zero violating mass" holds for its seeds 0-4 only.
7. **MINT's single cost line is a poor fit at small ek** (+28-39% at ek = 100 on LAION-1M-4,
   +69-78% on Flickr30k-4); C4 uses piecewise-linear numDist. Warm start sometimes ends cheaper
   than cold (Flickr30k-4: 0.24-0.51% on 5 of 5 seeds), since Algorithm 2 is a local search.

## 6. Deviations from the brief and Reply 1

- C4 deviations from MINT, fixed before the run (`C4_DESIGN.md`): exact full-table ranks for a
  query sample instead of a 1% data sample; models fitted on the full single-column indexes;
  piecewise-linear numDist and a monotone planner for |X_q| > 3 (both corrections from the smoke
  test, before any timed run); search continues through infeasible configurations.
- P3 faithfulness on LAION-1M-4 (4 columns, at most 15 distinct queries) rather than MINT's
  8-column semi-synthetic table; 7 of 15 MINT-construction workloads had no feasible
  configuration within 4 indexes (timing still valid).
- P3 warm-start cut on tuning time only (the stricter reading); including calibration it is
  98.2-100%.
- P2a query time is estimated (frozen simulator); tuner and build costs are measured inputs. The
  units are mixed by construction; the protocol states this.

## 7. Problems and open questions

1. **P2a failed as written. How is C2 to be framed?** Options: (a) keep C2 and state its regime
   (tuner cost per re-tune comparable to window query time); (b) re-frame C2 around stability
   (switches, false switches) with a cost model that charges builds against serving; (c) drop the
   detector claim and present FIR-MINT as warm start plus ski-rental. Recommendation: (b), with
   (a) stated as the regime of the total-time gain; it is what the data show.
2. **Readings to confirm (stated in the P2a protocol before the run):** (a) the gated tuner cost
   is C4's warm time; (b) the condition holds at every Q_w, not on average (on average, COCO-6 30k
   and Flickr30k-4 would pass; COCO-4 and COCO-6 full would still fail); (c) Continuous+SR keeps
   the rent account while its proposal is unchanged.
3. **P3 warm-start cut on tuning time only**: confirm this is the reading intended.
4. **P1b on LAION-1M-4**: C4's configuration does not move on that table (finding 5). If P1b uses
   C4, the LAION seeds will mostly be non-informative; if it uses v1.0's tuner (which moved on 3 of
   5 seeds in P1), the result is not about MINT's tuner. Which tuner does P1b use?
5. **Storage (D3)**: the 1 TB request to the cluster administrators is not yet sent; a draft can
   follow.
